About the Office of Alumni Affairs and Relations

The Office of the Alumni Affairs and Relations at ISAT U fosters lifelong connections between the university and its graduates. It builds relationships through regular communication, events, and engagement activities. The office encourages alumni involvement in mentoring, guest lecturing, and advisory roles, and maintains an up-to-date alumni database for effective outreach. It mobilizes resources for university projects, offers career services, and recognizes alumni achievements. By promoting university goals and values, the office enhances alumni pride and contributes to ISAT U's growth and success.

SUC Leveling

SUC Leveling is conducted to determine the classification level of a state university or college and assess its performance based on research, instruction, extension, and resource management.

Key Result Area (KRA 1)

Quality and Relevance of Instruction – 16 points

Item 5: Employability of Graduates

Mandate: Department of Budget and Management and Commission on Higher Education Joint Circular No. 1, s. 2016 (April 13, 2016), “FY 2016 Leveling Instrument for SUCs and Guidelines for Implementation Thereof”.

ISAT U Alumni Ambassadors

ISAT U Alumni Ambassadors — a dynamic group of passionate graduates dedicated to strengthening the bridge between the university and its alumni community.

Who are the Alumni Ambassadors?

They are proud representatives of Iloilo Science and Technology University – chosen for their leadership, integrity, and commitment to lifelong learning. These ambassadors embody the ISAT U spirit of excellence, innovation, and service.

Our Advocacy

The ISAT U Alumni Ambassadors aim to:

  • Reconnect alumni across generations to celebrate shared pride and achievements.
  • Inspire current students through mentorship, leadership, and real-world experiences.
  • Empower the ISAT U community by promoting alumni engagement, professional growth, and community service.
  • Uphold the values of ISAT U — excellence, service, and innovation — wherever we go.
